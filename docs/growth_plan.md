# Growth Plan: 500 registrations in 7 days on ₹2,000

**Workshop:** "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes" (free, online)
**Core idea:** ₹2,000 can't *buy* 500 final-year students, so students have to *bring* each other. The plan is a campus-ambassador seed plus a referral loop, measured daily on one dashboard.

---

## 1. The student

**Who:** Final-year (2027 batch) B.Tech students in CSE, IT, AI/ML, ECE at tier-2/3 engineering colleges in AP, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka — NxtWave's core geography. Placement season is starting for them right now (Oct–Dec).

**Why they'd care:** Their resume has no AI project, and every JD they see now mentions AI. Most have watched AI tutorials but never shipped anything. Their real fear is "the interviewer asks what I built, and I have nothing to show."

**What makes them register:**
- A concrete outcome: a deployed project + GitHub link in 60 minutes, not "learn about AI"
- Trust: the invite comes from a classmate in their batch WhatsApp group, not an ad
- Low risk: free, online, one hour, on a Sunday
- Mild FOMO: a live seat counter (1,000-seat webinar) and "11 students from your college registered"

**What stops them:** "Another webinar that's a sales pitch." The copy promises a build, and the share message comes from a peer.

---

## 2. The campaign: three channels, in priority order

| # | Channel | What we do | Expected regs |
|---|---------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Campus ambassadors** | Recruit 25 students (from NxtWave's existing learner community + tech-club leads DM'd on LinkedIn) on Day 0–1. Each posts in their class/batch WhatsApp groups with their personal link. Groups have 60–120 students; we assume ~10 regs per ambassador across 3 posts. | **~220** |
| 2 | **Referral loop** (the built asset) | Every registrant gets a personal link + one-tap WhatsApp share + a reward ladder: 1 friend → recording & prompt pack, 3 → certificate & Q&A priority, 10 → 1:1 mentor review. College-vs-college leaderboard. Assumptions: 28% share, 1.5 friends each. | **~160** |
| 3 | **Clubs & placement cells** | Message 30 coding clubs / placement coordinators with a ready-to-forward note ("good for your batch before placements"). | **~60** |
| — | Long tail | LinkedIn posts by ambassadors, Telegram/Discord groups, 2 emails to past NxtWave leads | **~60** |
| | **Total** | | **~500** |

**Budget (₹2,000):** All of it goes to the top 3 referrers as prizes: ₹1,000 / ₹600 / ₹400 vouchers. It's paid only for results and creates competition at the top of the leaderboard. Every other reward costs nothing (recording, certificate, mentor time).

**Why not paid ads?** Assuming a ₹20–30 cost per lead for student-targeted Meta ads (an assumption to verify), ₹2,000 buys roughly 70–100 registrations, with weak final-year targeting. The same money as prizes can drive a much larger number of peer referrals.

## 4. How we'll know it's working

The dashboard tracks pacing vs. this plan, conversion per channel, K-factor (new users each registrant brings), share rate, and % final-year. Decision rules:
- **Behind plan on Day 3:** recruit 10 more ambassadors in the best-performing colleges
- **K-factor below 0.25:** move the 3-friend reward into the welcome WhatsApp message
- **Final-year % below 70%:** stop club posts that skew junior; focus on 2027 groups
- **One channel converts 2× others:** move ambassador effort there

**Biggest risk:** registered ≠ attended. Free webinars often see well under half of registrants show up (to verify against NxtWave's past events). Mitigation: an "Add to Google Calendar" button right after registering, reminders at T-24h and T-1h through the n8n hook, and "bring a laptop, you'll build live" to raise commitment.

*Note: all numbers are planning assumptions. The dashboard demo runs on simulated data generated from these assumptions.*
