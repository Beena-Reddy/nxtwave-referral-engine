"₹2,000 and 7 days for 500 final-year students. That budget can't buy 500 people with ads. So my plan is to make students bring each other, and I built the engine that does that."

Final-years in placement season with no AI project on their resume. Three channels: 25 campus ambassadors (~220), the referral loop (~160), clubs and placement cells (~60), plus a long tail. All ₹2,000 goes to top-3 referrer prizes.

**1:05–2:15 — Live demo**
1. Open an ambassador's link: "Medaram Vishnu Vardhan from Amrita invited you".
2. Register (show the duplicate check too: same number with +91 goes back to the same page) → personal link, WhatsApp share, Add to Google Calendar (because registered isn't attended), reward ladder.
3. "Write my message" → pick club group / Telugu.
4. Register a friend through the link → count goes to 1, first tier unlocks.
5. Leaderboard: college vs college.
6. Dashboard: pacing vs plan, channel conversion, K-factor, peak hour, and the auto-insights. Say clearly that this is simulated data from my plan's assumptions.

**2:15–2:45 — How I thought**
What I changed from my first idea, one thing AI suggested that I rejected and why.

**2:45–3:00 — Next 24 hours**
Connect the n8n webhook (already built) for welcome emails and reminders, a waitlist after 500, referral fraud checks, attendance tracking.
